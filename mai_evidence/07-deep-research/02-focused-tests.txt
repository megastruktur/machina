## Fix under test

Reviewer FAIL on a933359: the logs step computed the artifact handle
list from `host.logs` outcomes and discarded it — `SourceEvidence` had
no artifact-ref field, the logs `StepRecord.evidence_refs` was empty,
nothing propagated into the promoted incident document, and no test
pinned the docstring's "read-only, artifact-referenced" claim.

Fix (allowlisted files only):

- `backend/src/mai/research/workflow.py`
  - `SourceEvidence` gains `artifact_refs: tuple[str, ...] = ()`
    (opaque `artifact:<path>#<sha256>` handles; empty when the producer
    returned no artifact).
  - New module helper `_log_artifact_refs(document)` — the single
    extraction of the handle from a parsed `host.logs` document.
  - `_record_capability` gains `refs_from_document`; on success the
    handle becomes the step record's `evidence_refs`, so the incident
    payload built from settled steps (C07 rule 5) carries it unchanged.
  - Logs phase attaches the same handle to the `SourceEvidence` and
    passes `refs_from_document=_log_artifact_refs`.
  - `report_document` serializes `artifact_refs` per source;
    `render_report` lists them under the source heading (steps already
    rendered their `evidence_refs`).
- `backend/tests/test_research_workflow.py` — 2 new tests (below).

## New tests

- `TestRunComposition::test_log_artifact_handles_preserved_on_source_step_and_report`
  — succeeded run with one `host.logs` query: asserts the handle
  `artifact:evidence/litellm.journal#<sha256 ab*32>` on (1) the
  `host_logs` `SourceEvidence.artifact_refs`, (2) the logs
  `StepRecord.evidence_refs`, (3) `report_document(...)["sources"]`
  JSON, and (4) the rendered Markdown (both the source bullet and the
  `## Steps` section).
- `TestIncidentPromotion::test_log_artifact_handle_propagates_into_promoted_incident`
  — same logs query plus a synthesizer that raises: the run fails and
  promotes a canonical incident through the real DocGenerator +
  DocumentStore; asserts the settled logs step still carries the handle
  and the stored incident document text cites it (`generate.py` renders
  step `evidence_refs`).
